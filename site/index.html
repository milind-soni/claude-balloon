Speak a task.
Watch it float.

A tiny Mac toy for Claude agents. Hold the helium tank's nozzle and say what you want done — a balloon inflates with your voice, drifts across your desktop while the agent works, and pops when it's finished, dropping the result. That's it. That's the app.

The balloon swells with your voice. Transcribed on-device by whisper — no audio ever leaves your Mac.

A real Claude Code agent runs your task headlessly. The balloon floats on your desktop; double-click it to watch the agent think.
